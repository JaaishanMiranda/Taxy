## How this project adapts it

- **Colour:** `#087cf8` is used only for large text and decoration (4.0:1 on white). Buttons and links use
  `#0a62c9` (5.8:1), and the hero gradient stays dark (`#0a508f` → `#0a62c9`) behind the text. `#8ad8fc` is
  decoration only. `#ac6853` becomes the warm Privacy accent, with `#8a4f3d` for its text (6.4:1).
- **Type:** Inter (variable, self-hosted). The heading ratio and tight tracking are kept, but the body is
  16px instead of 12px, to meet NFR9.
- **Container:** 710px is used for hero copy; sections run to about 1160px.
